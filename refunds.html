Refund & cancellation

RecruitFlow uses monthly subscriptions with no one-time licence payment.

Cancel anytime

You may cancel renewal through RecruitFlow’s billing controls or by emailing TaigenDamonRay@gmail.com. Cancel before the next renewal date to avoid the next monthly charge. Unless law requires otherwise, access continues to the end of the paid period.

Refund requests

Monthly fees are generally non-refundable once a billing period has begun because service access and infrastructure are made available immediately. We will review duplicate charges, unauthorised charges, verified billing errors and extended service failures attributable to RecruitFlow. This policy does not limit any cooling-off, cancellation, refund or other consumer right that applies under law.

How to request help

Email us within 14 days of the charge and include the account email, charge date, amount and reason for the request. Do not email full card details. We aim to acknowledge billing requests within two business days and, where approved, submit refunds to Stripe promptly. The bank or card network controls when the credit appears.

Plan changes

Upgrades or downgrades may be prorated or take effect at the next billing period as shown in Stripe’s checkout or billing portal. Review the displayed amount before confirming a change.

Billing disputes

Please contact us before initiating a payment dispute so we can investigate quickly. Nothing in this request prevents you from exercising rights available through your bank, card issuer or applicable law.
