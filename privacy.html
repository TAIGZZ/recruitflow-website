Privacy Policy

RecruitFlow is operated by Taigen Damon Ray, a sole proprietor trading as RecruitFlow in South Africa. This policy explains how we handle personal information under applicable privacy laws, including South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act.

Privacy contact
recruitflowhq.support@gmail.com

1. Scope and roles

We are the responsible party for account, billing, website and support information collected directly from customers. When a recruitment business uses RecruitFlow to process candidate, client or employee information, that customer determines why and how that information is used and is generally the responsible party; RecruitFlow acts as its service provider or operator.

2. Information we process

  • Account identity, authentication identifiers and workspace membership.
  • Subscription, billing status and payment-related identifiers. Complete card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by RecruitFlow.
  • Support communications, diagnostic information and audit events.
  • Recruitment records supplied by customers, which may include candidate contact details, CVs, work history, qualifications, notes, interview information and client records.
  • Basic website request information required to deliver and secure the website.

3. Why we process information

We process information to provide and secure the service, authenticate users, maintain recruitment workspaces, administer subscriptions, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, comply with law and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

4. Lawful basis

Processing may be necessary to perform a contract, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate business and security interests, or act on consent where consent is required. Customers are responsible for establishing an appropriate lawful basis for candidate and client information they place in RecruitFlow.

5. Service providers and international processing

We use carefully selected providers for authentication, payments, application hosting and website delivery, including Auth0, Stripe, Render and GitHub Pages. These providers may process information outside South Africa. We use contractual, security and access controls appropriate to the service and information involved.

6. Retention

We retain account and operational information only while reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, contractual obligations, dispute handling and legal recordkeeping. Customers control retention of recruitment records in their workspaces and should configure, export or delete them according to their own legal obligations.

7. Security

RecruitFlow uses access controls, tenant separation, secure authentication, audit history, restricted processing and backups designed to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Customers must safeguard their accounts, devices and exported files and notify us promptly of suspected unauthorised access.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of or objection to processing of your personal information. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. Candidate requests concerning information controlled by a recruitment agency should normally be directed to that agency first.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the service develops or legal requirements change. Material changes will be identified by an updated effective date and, where appropriate, communicated in the service.

10. Contact

Email privacy and data-rights requests to recruitflowhq.support@gmail.com. We may need to verify identity and authority before releasing or changing personal information.
