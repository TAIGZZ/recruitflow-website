07

Questions before adopting

Clear answers,
before you commit.

Is RecruitFlow an applicant tracking system?

It is a focused recruitment operations workspace. It covers positions, candidates, submissions, interviews, placements, fees and follow-ups without the complexity of a large enterprise ATS.

Do automations send emails without approval?

No. RecruitFlow prepares drafts for review. Rules can also require approval before their configured actions run.

Does RecruitFlow currently use AI?

The production automation engine is rule-based. Agency AI is an early-access tier for future assisted workflows; the website does not present unfinished AI capabilities as available today.

Can I work outside South Africa?

Yes. The workspace supports multiple currencies and regional formats, and the product is positioned for recruiters and agencies globally.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Manual and automatic backups are built into the Windows app, with restore controls and configurable retention.
