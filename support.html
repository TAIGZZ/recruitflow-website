Product support

For installation, sign-in, backups, automations or workspace issues, describe what you expected, what happened and any visible error message. Do not send candidate CVs, passwords, authentication codes or full payment-card information by email.

Billing support

Include your account email, charge date and amount. Subscription management and approved refunds are processed securely through Stripe.

Privacy requests

Use the subject “Privacy request” for access, correction, deletion or objection requests. We may need to verify your identity and authority. If your request concerns information held by a recruitment agency using RecruitFlow, contact that agency first because it normally controls the recruitment record.

Security reports

Use the subject “Security report” and avoid including sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to identify the issue. We will prioritise credible reports affecting account or recruitment-data security.
